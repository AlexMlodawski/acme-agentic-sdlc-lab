# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not people. Do not
harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose private information, or use project
spaces for spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
necessary to keep the community safe. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers through the repository host's private contact mechanism.
