Acme Store Return Policy (fictional lab data)

This policy applies only to the fictional Acme Support Portal demonstration.

1. Standard returns
Customers may request a standard return within 30 calendar days after delivery. The item should include its supplied parts and accessories. Eligibility is confirmed only after the order and item condition are reviewed.

2. Opened products
An opened product may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery when it is complete, undamaged, and in suitable condition for inspection. Opening a product does not by itself guarantee or prevent a refund.

3. Damaged or incorrect products
A damaged product or a product shipped in error should be reported within 48 hours after delivery. After the case is verified, Acme provides a prepaid return label for a damaged or incorrectly shipped product.

4. Exclusions
Personalized products and items marked final sale are not eligible for a standard return. Support may still review a report that an item arrived damaged or was shipped in error; no outcome should be promised before verification.

5. Refund decisions
The policy does not authorize an automatic promise of a refund, refund amount, replacement, or processing date. Support must verify the order, timing, product condition, and applicable exclusion before confirming the available resolution.

6. Getting help
The Store Support Agent can explain this policy and help a customer decide whether using the support-case form is appropriate. The agent does not create a support case and does not approve a refund. Customers who need individual review should use the portal support-case form.
